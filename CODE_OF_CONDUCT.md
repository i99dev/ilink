# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in this project should be able to contribute without harassment or discrimination. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully across backgrounds, identities, experience levels and viewpoints.
- Discuss code and ideas constructively. Explain concerns, accept corrections and make room for questions.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only information you have permission to disclose.
- Keep contributions and discussions relevant to the project, and respect its vehicle-safety requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruptive behavior and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Retaliating against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact a project maintainer privately using a contact method they have made available. Include relevant context and links when safe to do so; avoid posting private evidence or identifying information in public issues. If the concern involves one maintainer, contact another maintainer where available.

Maintainers will review reports, limit disclosure to what is needed to handle the concern, and take proportionate action. Actions may include requesting a change in behavior, removing content, restricting participation temporarily or excluding a participant from project spaces. Maintainers are subject to these expectations too.

For software vulnerabilities, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting option in the repository's Security tab if available; otherwise contact a maintainer privately. See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development and reporting guidance.
